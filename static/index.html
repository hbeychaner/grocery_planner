Google Home & IFTTT Setup Guide

You can connect your Google Home / Google Assistant to this application in 2 minutes:

  1. Open IFTTT Applet Creator.
  2. If This: Choose Google Assistant → "Say a phrase with a text ingredient".
  3. Enter trigger phrases: Check price of $ or Compare price of $.
  4. Then That: Choose Webhooks → "Make a web request".
  5. Configure the webhook:
    • URL: https://<your-domain>/api/v1/voice/ifttt
    • Method: POST
    • Content-Type: application/json
    • Body: {"ingredient": "{{TextField}}"}
